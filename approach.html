My Approach

Understanding the whole of you

Rooted in psychodynamic thought, personalized to your needs, and holistic in spirit.

I value the multidimensional influences of life and personality, and acknowledge the interplay of conscious and unconscious dynamics and defenses in shaping our life experiences and relationships.

Psychodynamic foundation

My therapeutic approach is based on psychoanalytic and psychodynamic theories, concepts, and techniques. I believe that understanding, bringing to awareness, and processing the interplay of internal conflicts, unconscious defenses, and socio-spiritual influences are central to therapeutic healing and growth.

Personalized care

Personalizing therapeutic interventions to meet individual patient needs is strengthened by incorporating strategies from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Dialectical Behavior Therapy, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, Narrative Therapy, and mindfulness-based approaches.

Trauma & EMDR

Trauma significantly and subtly affects personality and self-development, shaping an individual's life experiences. I am professionally trained in the EMDR approach to address and alleviate the effects of both major and complex traumas.

Integrative methods

Approaches I draw from

  • Psychoanalytic Therapy
  • Psychodynamic Therapy
  • EMDR
  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)
  • Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)
  • Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT)
  • Narrative Therapy
  • Mindfulness-based approaches
My methodology incorporates a holistic perspective, recognizing the significance of both physical and spiritual well-being as integral to psychological health.

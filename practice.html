Services

Individual psychotherapy

Psychodynamic and psychoanalytically informed therapy to explore internal conflicts, unconscious patterns, relationships, and sense of self.

Trauma therapy & EMDR

Care for the effects of both major and complex trauma, using Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR).

Integrative, holistic care

Strategies from CBT, DBT, ACT, Narrative Therapy, and mindfulness, tailored to you — honoring physical and spiritual well-being as part of psychological health.

Getting started

What to expect

1

Read the Welcome Note

Begin on the New Patients page by reading and acknowledging the Welcome Note.

2

Request an appointment

Share your contact details and preferred times. The practice will reach out to arrange a first session.

3

Begin the journey

Your first sessions focus on understanding your story, your needs, and the goals we set together.

Emergencies: This practice does not provide emergency services. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), call 911, or go to the nearest emergency room.

Privacy: Please avoid sending detailed clinical information through the website or by email.

About Me

Hema Babaria, Ph.D.

Psychologist · EMDR Trained Therapist · Chester Springs, Pennsylvania

Hello,

My name is Hema Babaria.

I am a professional psychologist with cultural roots and ancestry in India, and I am currently settled in Pennsylvania, USA. I am multilingual and proficient in English.

I earned my Doctorate in Psychology (Ph.D.) from Saurashtra University in Gujarat, India. My certification as a psychologist in Pennsylvania began with my work at the Institute for Behavior Change in Exton. I then completed a Post-Doctoral Fellowship at the Counseling Center at Rutgers University in Camden, New Jersey. Additionally, I am certified as an Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) Trained Therapist.

Currently, I am a member of the Psychology Department at the St. John Vianney Center in Downingtown, PA, and operate a private practice located in Chester Springs, PA.

Welcome Note

Welcome to my practice!

Life is a uniquely experienced journey, allowing us to mold, build, grow, and develop our physical, emotional, interpersonal, and spiritual selves. Each moment resonates with the music created by the interplay between our internal self and the external world. We all connect with, nurture, and play this tune in our own ways to flourish in both our emotional, social, and spiritual environments.

The process of therapy is also a journey—one that is unique and individualized. It is an opportunity to explore, understand, connect, reflect, grow, mature, nurture, and enrich our sense of self. Therapy facilitates engagement with both conscious and unconscious aspects of the emotional, intellectual, social, and spiritual realms of the internal self, to work through the pain and overcome anchors that impede progress and healing.

Let us take this journey together.

— Hema Babaria, Ph.D.
